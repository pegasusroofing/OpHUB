Why OpHUB

Built around the people actually on site

OpHUB was built with one focus: giving site operatives a single, clear destination for everything a job needs — not another system to fight with. It's just as easy for project managers too, taking away the need for multiple systems by bringing it all into one operative-friendly app. Think of it as a job folder in an app, with no ties to any commercial software. Originally designed for on-site roofing teams, OpHUB has since grown to support every trade on site.

Everything in one place

One app for the whole site team

Set it up once, brand it as your own, and give every operative, driver, PM and admin exactly what they need.

📍

GPS Check-in

Geofenced check-in and check-out, so you always know who's actually on site.

📋

Schedule of Works

Track every task to-commence, in progress or complete — with photo evidence attached.

🛡

Health & Safety

RAMS and Toolbox Talks signed digitally by every operative before starting — a clean, searchable audit trail, so you're never scrambling when a client's dreaded Friday pack request lands.

🏷

Snagging / Variations

Log, photograph and close snags or variations on the spot, then export a PDF report — live, closed or both.

🚚

Delivery Scheduler

Plan the week's deliveries and collections slot by slot, assign them to a driver, and get notified with photos the moment each drop is completed.

🚐

Driver App

Drivers get their own simple view: today's runs in order, what to collect from where, site contacts, photo sign-off and a weekly vehicle checklist.

🧱

Material Orders

Operatives request materials from site; you approve, email the order straight to your supplier, and choose delivery or collection — it drops onto the delivery schedule automatically.

📐

Drawings & Programme

Upload and organise drawings, specs and your construction programme — PDF, Excel, JPEG or PNG — with folder-level visibility controls and automatic "supersedes/superseded by" tracking, so the latest revision is always the one people see.

📳

HAVS Monitoring

Log daily hand-arm vibration exposure per operative and tool, with automatic weekly summaries and a clear warning before anyone approaches the action or exposure limit.

☁️

OneDrive Sync & Exports

Progress photos and drawings sync straight to your company's OneDrive, and Schedule of Works, Snagging/Variations and Health & Safety sign-offs all export as clean, ready-to-send PDF reports you can view instantly, right inside the app.

💬

Team Messaging

Broadcast updates to operatives or a site's project manager, with in-app notifications and unread messages that stand out.

✅

PM To Do Lists

A simple to-do list for every site — add a job, optionally assign it to a project manager, and tick it off once it's closed.

📝

Custom Report Templates

Build your own report templates once from a central Admin Centre, then fill, duplicate and export them for any site in seconds.

🏢

Multi-Site Admin

Manage every site, team member and responsible project manager from one dashboard.

🎨

Your Branding

Set your own logo and colour theme — it feels like your own company's app, not ours.
