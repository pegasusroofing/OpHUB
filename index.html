Why OpHUB

Built around the people actually on site

OpHUB was built with one focus: giving site operatives a single, clear destination for everything a job needs — not another system to fight with. It's just as easy for project managers too, taking away the need for multiple systems by bringing it all into one operative-friendly app. Think of it as a job folder in an app, with no ties to any commercial software. Originally designed for on-site roofing teams, OpHUB has since grown to support every trade on site.

👷

Peace of mind for management

Everything in one place

One app for the whole site team

From check-in to handover — set it up once, brand it as your own, and give every operative, driver, PM and admin exactly what they need.

📍

GPS Check-in

Geofenced check-in and check-out — including multi-address jobs — so you always know who's on site.

📋

Schedule of Works

Sections and tasks with progress and photos, built from your own library or imported, exported as a branded PDF.

🛡

RAMS & COSHH builders

Build RAMS from your hazard library, and turn a safety data sheet into a COSHH assessment automatically. Signed digitally by every operative.

🧪

COSHH Library

Assessments filed in folders, shared across jobs, with yearly review reminders — tick several to export or email.

🗣

Toolbox Talks & Briefings

Live toolbox talks and daily briefings signed on the phone, plus inductions, site rules and your H&S policy.

⚠️

PPE, Incidents & Inspections

PPE issue log, accident and near-miss reports, HAVS, PUWER, LOLER and permits to work.

🏷

Snagging / Variations

Log and photograph snags or variations, mark up the photo, close out with evidence and export a PDF.

📐

Drawings & Programme

Drawings, specs and programmes in folders — superseded versions kept, mark-up on the plan.

📝

Reports & Surveys

Your own report templates, filled on site with photos and signatures — even with no signal.

🧱

Materials & Suppliers

Operatives request materials; you approve and email the order to the right supplier branch.

🚚

Delivery Scheduler

Plan the day's drops and collections slot by slot and assign them to drivers.

🚐

Driver App

Drivers see today's runs in order, take completion photos and mark them up, and do their weekly vehicle checks.

🔧

Repairs & Maintenance

Repair surveys with photos, approval, closed subject to invoice, then invoiced — photos synced to OneDrive.

📘

O&M Handover

When a job closes, build the O&M manual from the job — data sheets, COSHH, guarantees, maintenance — and export to PDF or Word.

🌴

HR & Holidays

Holiday requests and approvals with bank holidays booked automatically, plus policies and the staff handbook.

👷

Operatives & Training

Every operative's cards, certificates and expiry dates in a training matrix.

🏢

Main Contractor Mode

Run subcontractors, their operatives, RAMS, COSHH and inspections when you're the main contractor.

📅

Calendars & Dashboards

Key dates per job, a company calendar, and a dashboard across every project.

💬

HUB & Messaging

One inbox for messages, sign-offs and alerts, with push notifications.

☁️

OneDrive Sync & Exports

Photos, drawings and exports sync straight to each project's OneDrive folder.

🎨

Your Branding

Your logo and colours throughout the app and every PDF — it feels like your own app.
