Why OpHUB

Built around the people actually on site

OpHUB was built with one focus — giving site operatives a platform that's concise and clear, not another system to fight with. That's peace of mind for the management team too: everything lives in one place, with no ties to any commercial software. Think of it as a job folder in an app. Originally designed for on-site roofing teams, OpHUB has since grown to support every trade on site.
