OpHUB OFFLINE DEMO (no internet needed)

This is the new app with a pretend company. Nothing you do in it touches RTB's real data.
What you change is kept on that laptop or phone. To start again, add ?reset-demo to the address.

ON THE LAPTOP
  1. Double-click "Start OpHUB Offline.bat" in this folder.
  2. The browser opens at http://localhost:8099. Leave the black window open while you use it.
  3. Close the black window to stop it.

ON THE PHONE (do this BEFORE the flight, with signal)
  1. Open https://ophub-offline-demo.vercel.app in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Wait about 30 seconds on the sign-in page while it saves itself onto the phone.
  3. iPhone: Share > Add To Home Screen. Android: menu > Add To Home Screen.
  4. Still with signal, open "OpHUB Demo" from the home screen once and wait 30 seconds on the sign-in page
     (on an iPhone the home-screen icon keeps its own copy, separate from Safari).
  5. Test it: turn on Airplane Mode, close the app fully, open "OpHUB Demo" again and sign in.
  6. On the plane, open "OpHUB Demo" from the home screen.

Not offline: maps, sending emails, Xero and OneDrive (they need the internet). Photos show a sample picture.

To rebuild after changes: cd app-native, npx expo export --platform web --output-dir ../offline/app,
then node tools/offline/make-offline.js. To update the phone link: copy offline/app to a folder named
ophub-offline-demo and run npx vercel deploy --prod --yes --scope op-hub in it (Vercel project ophub-offline-demo).
Check it: DEMO=https://ophub-offline-demo.vercel.app node tools/offline/offline-check.js
