AI that does the admin

The paperwork reads itself

OpHUB's AI reads the invoices, writes the RAMS and keeps the price book up to date, so your team checks rather than types.

Invoice Reader

Supplier invoices land in your inbox. OpHUB reads them, finds the PO and the job, and puts them in front of the right person to approve.

RAMS Builder

A site-specific RAMS from the job, the task and your own library, ready to sign on the phone.

COSHH From Data Sheets

Upload a safety data sheet and the COSHH assessment is written for you.

Price List Reader

Supplier price lists in Excel or PDF become your price book, so every PO is priced before the invoice arrives.

Statement Matching

Supplier statements matched to the invoices you have, with anything missing flagged.

Quote Reader

Quotes read into line items you can order from, line by line.
