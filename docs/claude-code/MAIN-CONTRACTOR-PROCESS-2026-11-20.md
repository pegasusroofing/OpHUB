# Main contractor and subcontractors in app.operativehub (20 Nov 2026)

How it works today when RTB is the main contractor, step by step. Then what's missing, for us to walk through together.

## 1. Make the job a main contractor job
- **Who:** a PM or admin.
- **Where:** the job › Project Settings › **Main Contractor** › tick **Acting as a Main Contractor?**
- The job home then gets a **Main Contractor** row with 9 tiles:
  - Construction Phase Plan
  - Site Rules & Layout
  - Site Inductions
  - Subcontractors
  - Permits To Work
  - Inspections
  - Asbestos Register
  - F10 Notification
  - Programme
- Each tile can be hidden from operatives (⋮ › Hide from operatives).

## 2. The Construction Phase Plan
- Build it in the CPP builder: Details · People · Plan · Emergency · Issue. It saves by itself, with **Next** at the bottom of every tab.
- Issue makes the PDF, files it in the job's CPP folder and keeps each version.
- The company's starting wording is in Admin Centre › H&S › CPP Template. That now saves by itself too.
- The O&M now takes the people, dates and outline of works from the CPP (see 8).

## 3. Add the subcontractors
- **Who:** a PM, admin or site manager.
- **Where:** Main Contractor › Subcontractors › **+ Add Subcontractor**. You type the company name.
- **Before the job (optional):** the subcontractor fills in the sign-up form.
  - Admin Centre › Subcontractor sign-up gives you a link to send them.
  - You approve it, or send it back, under the New / Approved / Sent Back tabs.

## 4. Their people
- Subcontractor staff are ordinary **operative** logins in our company. They join with our invite link.
- **Allocate them:** on the company's Operatives tile (or job Settings › Team).
- **Make one their admin:** tick **Make this their subcontractor admin**. That gives them upload rights for their company.
- **What their operatives see:** on the job, they go straight to their company's page. Its tiles are:
  - RAMS
  - COSHH
  - TBT
  - Schedule of Works
  - Permits To Work
  - Inspections
  - Daily Briefings
  - Operatives
  - **O&M Manual** (new, for their admin)
- **What their operatives do:**
  - complete the site induction;
  - sign their company's RAMS and COSHH, and the site rules and layout;
  - sign toolbox talks;
  - update the Schedule of Works tasks given to them;
  - check in and out.

## 5. Their paperwork
- **What their admin (or our managers) can upload to their company folder:**
  - RAMS, COSHH, toolbox talks and daily briefings;
  - inspection files (we're emailed when one is added).
- **What we see:** their RAMS and COSHH appear on our H&S pages under each company. Their toolbox talks appear under "Subcontractor Toolbox Talks".
- **Insurance and accreditations:** kept in Admin Centre › Company Library, uploaded by our managers, with expiry dates.

## 6. Running the site
- **Permits To Work:**
  - The four types are Hot Works, Working at Height, Asbestos (ACM) and Hazardous Areas.
  - We or their admin issue them; operatives sign.
  - To close one, hot works needs two photos.
- **Inspections:** filled in from our inspection templates.
- **Work:** given out with Schedule of Works › task ⋮ › **Assign to subcontractor**.
- **Their orders and payments:** Materials › Subcontract Orders, and Finance › Subcontractors.

## 7. Their O&M manuals (new today)
- Their admin opens **O&M Manual** on their company page, then either:
  - **⬆️ Upload Our O&M (PDF)**, which is submitted straight away; or
  - **+ Build One Here**, in the same O&M builder.
- **A manual they build here:**
  - starts with only the Schedule of Works tasks given to them, their photos and their COSHH;
  - has their company as the contractor and us as their client;
  - **Submit Revision 1** on its Issue tab sends it to us.
- **What we see:**
  - Handover lists **Subcontractors' O&M Manuals** with a View button for each.
  - Our O&M's Appendix tab has **Subcontractors' O&M Manuals (N Submitted)**, ticked. When we issue, their PDFs go in the back of ours.
- Only their admin and our managers can see them. This is checked on the database.

## 8. Handover (our O&M)
- **Where:** Handover › O&M Manuals › + New O&M Manual. The builder is now in tabs: Details · People · Works · Appendix · Issue.
- **What comes from the CPP:**
  - the client and their contact;
  - the client's agent, principal designer, architect and project manager;
  - start on site and the outline of works.
- **Issue:**
  - checks what's missing first;
  - offers practical completion as today if it isn't set;
  - makes Revision 1, 2… and keeps each PDF.

## What's missing: to walk through together
1. **The Handover row only appears once a job is closed.** But closing the job shuts the subcontractors out, so they must submit their O&Ms **before** you close it. *Suggest:* show Handover on live main contractor jobs too, or warn when closing a job with subcontractors who haven't submitted.
2. **Nothing tells us when a subcontractor submits their O&M.** *Suggest:* a HUB item for the job's PM, and the same for new RAMS and COSHH.
3. **We can't approve their paperwork.** RAMS, COSHH, toolbox talks and briefings are live as soon as they're uploaded. *Suggest:* Accepted / Send Back on each, like the sign-up form.
4. **Approving a sign-up doesn't set anything up.** It doesn't create the company, link it to a job, or copy their insurance into the Company Library. *Suggest:* **Approve** also adds them to the Company Library, and **Add Subcontractor** offers approved companies first.
5. **No insurance check.** Nothing stops us adding a company whose insurance has expired. *Suggest:* a warning when adding them, and on their tile.
6. **Their staff must have one of our operative logins.** There's no way to add a named person per job without an account. They also get our employee items, such as "Fill in your Subcontractor Form". *Decide:* keep this, or give subcontractors their own lighter login.
7. **Their operatives can't easily reach the rest of the job.** They're sent straight to their company page, so Check In, Calendar and other job pages are hard to reach. Worth a test on a real phone.
8. **The CPP isn't signed.** Subcontractors sign the site rules and layout, but not the Construction Phase Plan. *Decide:* should they confirm they've read it?
9. **Daily briefings are a log only.** Operatives don't sign them, and nothing flags a missed one.
10. **There's no subcontractor close-out.** There's no completion or sign-off per company, and no final account or retention link to their record.
