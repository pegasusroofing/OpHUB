# Overnight review, 3–4 Nov 2026

Two jobs were done overnight:
- every change made on 3 Nov was checked for bugs;
- every area Andy hasn't reviewed yet was compared with the old web app.

Everything was read in the code. Testing was look-only on the ZZ jobs, plus the button crawl on the pretend database. Nothing was sent, posted to Xero, or changed on real jobs.

## Fixed overnight

### Bugs in the 3 Nov changes
- **RAMS builder:** opening a draft or clicking through its tabs no longer saves anything by itself.
  - Old drafts only get the address and main contractor filled in where they're blank, and only on screen until the PM edits.
  - A draft with no address can no longer crash.
- **RAMS / COSHH "View" on a phone** opens the document again. It had been going to the share sheet. On the laptop it still shows the signed copy with Issued By.
- **Email (H&S) and Revise RAMS** now show for PMs and admins only. Site managers saw them, but the email sender and the builder refuse site managers.
- **A supplier chosen on an unread inbox invoice** is now kept in its own column (`supplier_override`, added). Before, choosing it would have stopped the invoice ever being read automatically.
- **Project chosen on an unallocated row:** if a person chose it, that project wins over the one read off the invoice.
- **Date headings** on the PO and purchase-invoice tables now sort by date. Before, they sorted by the day number.
- **PDF panel:**
  - the page stays put when you switch to Full and back;
  - the PO's PDF refreshes after a change without reloading the whole page;
  - photos open as photos;
  - PDFs made in the browser are let go afterwards.
- **Tick-filters in tables** match whole words. "Paid" no longer also shows "Part paid".
- **COSHH "Save & Issue To This Job"** no longer leaves Create COSHH behind it when you press Back.
- **Chip text** on company-colour chips is now readable whatever the company colour is (COSHH product form, RAMS builder, COSHH library).

### Old app vs new app
- **Subcontractor PO page** could crash the first time it opened (a look-up was in the wrong place). Fixed.
- **"Check Xero for payments"** always said "nothing new" because it read the wrong answer. Fixed.
- **Emailed PDFs** now report a failure the server sends back, instead of saying "Emailed". This applies on every screen that emails a PDF.
- **Site inductions** are emailed through the induction email function with the stored signed PDF, as the old app did. The new app had been making a second copy of the health answers.
- **Incidents:** a date with no time no longer stops the report from submitting.
- **Yard stock:**
  - PMs and admins without Finance can add a new yard item from the job's Yard Stock again;
  - a return can carry a note;
  - the platform admin viewing a company counts as a yard manager.
- **Material requests:** a quantity picked from a project quote keeps its unit ("5 m").
- **Expenses:** a receipt not emailed yet now has "Send Now". It had been sent as "resent".
- **Operative invoices** have the "Post to Xero" row again.
- **Laptop card lists** (plant hire, unallocated / with a PM, statements) keep a slim search box. Those lists have no table headings to search by, and hiding the filter row had left them with no way to search.
- **Older RAMS** that already had signature boxes still count as multi-page signing. Their Signature boxes and Test fill options show again.
- **Unallocated invoice counts** on the Overview and the Purchases tile now match the list. Unread invoices count too.
- **Finance reports** sort by clicking a heading; click again to reverse. The Excel and PDF follow the order.
- **COSHH:** emailing several ticked COSHH asks "separate PDFs or one", as before.
- **Draft applications** keep in step with the Variations and Day works registers:
  - a rejected one comes off;
  - a changed approved value is updated;
  - ones added on the application and not approved yet carry on, as Andy set on 3 Nov.
- **PPE:** the job's PPE page links to the company PPE register.
- **Operative invoices:** an approved one can be marked paid today, without the pay run.

## Differences found but not changed (need Andy's say, or bigger work)

### Projects
- **New project form** is stricter than the old one. Address, Client and a Start date are required even for an Active job; the old app only needed the name. Should they be optional again?
- **Client contact name:** there's no field for it when creating a project. It can still be set later on Client Info.
- **"Set Active"** saves `active` where the old app saved `live`. Both are treated as Live everywhere, so nothing breaks.
- **Material request quantity** is numbers only in the new app; the old app allowed free text like "2 packs". Was that intended?
- **H&S policy:** site managers can't see superseded versions any more. Those are only in Admin › H&S Policy, which is for PMs and admins.
- **Daily briefings:** operatives can't open a briefing that's still being filled in. The database probably blocks this anyway.
- **Price sheet email and PDF** use the general document email and a plain table, not the old custom layout.
- **Subcontractor RAMS emails** from the new Export / Email row go through the new H&S sender (`send-hs-email`), which hasn't sent a real email yet.

### Finance
- **Xero**, all part of the Xero plan for today:
  - the "Ready to post" bulk list is missing;
  - the quiet payment pull when Finance opens is missing;
  - there's no "load accounts from Xero" picker;
  - there's no Post to Xero on a subcontractor application's payment.
- **New PO builder** (known from the earlier audit) is missing three things: a job on each line, a Discount % column, and numbered company cost codes like "310 – Materials".
- **Operative invoice page:** no "+ new person" while entering, and no reverse-charge VAT note. The "No UTR saved" warning is already there.
- **Purchase invoices / plant added by hand:** no plot or repair-job box when adding (it can be set afterwards). Labour is now out of the cost types, and a blank due date is now invoice date + 30 days.
- **Invoices typed in by hand** don't get the background line check against the PO. That waits for the reader, which is off during the trial.
- **PO, payment notice and pay-less notice emails** are still trial work.
- **Laptop tables** no longer have the "Value >1k" filter or ticking several suppliers or months at once. The table headings filter one value each.

## Button crawl (admin, laptop size, pretend database)
- 178 screens opened and about 4,400 buttons pressed.
- One real error: the purchase invoice add form had a broken line for a few minutes on the night (fixed in dd7cc16). The Costs and Monthly pages that showed it were re-crawled afterwards and are clean.
- No screen crashed. The 7 screens with no buttons are read-only pages (On Site Now, To Book, Getting Started and similar) and they show their text.
- The "could not press" items all come after a pop-up the crawler couldn't close. Pressing those buttons on their own works (checked on the PO page).
- **Worth knowing:** the Escape key doesn't close question pop-ups. Use the ✕ or No. That fits the rule that pop-ups close only on save, complete or exit.

- **Variation → Schedule of Works** finds its section by name, but the names are always unique (Variation 1, 2 …), so it can't link the wrong one.
