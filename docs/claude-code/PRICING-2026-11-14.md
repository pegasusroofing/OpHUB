# OpHUB pricing proposal (14 Nov 2026)

Andy asked: "figure out how much we can charge" for OpHUB Projects, OpHUB Finance and both together. Not on the website
yet (pricing is hidden for now). All prices per company, per month, excluding VAT, on a 12-month agreement.

What it tells us:
- Site apps that charge per user cost £37–£80 per person. A 25-person roofing firm would pay £900–£2,000/month.
  OpHUB's "no per-user fees" is a big selling point at that size.
- RAMS alone (HandsHQ) is £208+/month. OpHUB Projects includes RAMS, COSHH, toolbox talks and the rest.
- UK construction finance software starts around £500–£700/month and goes up fast. OpHUB Finance adds the AI invoice
  reader (Dext alone is £24–£59), Xero, and the live link from site.

## Proposed prices

| Plan | Who | Price |
|---|---|---|

| Pro | Up to 50 staff, 150 live sites | £399/month |
| Enterprise | Up to 150 staff, 500 live sites | £749/month |

### OpHUB Finance (priced by office users and live jobs)
| Plan | Who | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Finance Essentials | Up to 3 Finance users, 15 live jobs, 300 AI documents a month | £299/month |
| Finance Pro | Up to 8 Finance users, 60 live jobs, 1,000 AI documents a month | £549/month |
| Finance Enterprise | Unlimited Finance users, 250 live jobs, 3,000 AI documents a month | £899/month |

AI documents = invoices, statements, price lists and quotes read by the AI. A fair-use allowance keeps the running cost
covered; extra at £25 per 250.

### Projects + Finance (about 20% off the two together)
| Plan | Instead of | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Basic + Essentials | £498 | £399/month |
| Pro + Pro | £948 | £749/month |
| Enterprise + Enterprise | £1,648 | £1,299/month |

## Set-up, terms and offers
- One-off set-up: £495 Projects, £995 Finance (imports jobs, clients, suppliers, opening balances, the Xero link),
  £1,295 both. Waived with annual payment up front.
- 12-month agreement billed monthly; month-to-month is 20% more; pay annually and get 2 months free.
- Founding customers: the first 10 companies get 30% off for their first year in return for a case study and a reference.
- Super admin switches each company between OpHUB Projects, OpHUB Finance and Projects + Finance
  (Platform Admin › the company › Systems).

## Why these numbers
- A 25-person firm on Projects Basic pays £199, against £900+ on per-user apps: an easy yes, while still several times
  Site Samurai's entry price, which has far less in it.
- Finance Essentials at £299 sits under Evolution Mx and Archdesk's entry prices, with more in it (AI, Xero, site link).
- The bundle is the best value on purpose: Projects + Finance together is where OpHUB is different from everyone else.
