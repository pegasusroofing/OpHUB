5 — The WorkflowStart to paid. What moves it on, and who.
1. Set Up The Contract
New Project › Project Type: Repair Contract. Client, rates and invoicing (one per job, or monthly).
2. New Job From The Works Order
Upload the PDF (or several at once): job no., address, call-out, tenant and priority are read in. Lands in To Book.
3. Book In → Attend
Set the booking date (Booked In, on the calendar and the operative's app). The operative attends, adds photos and notes (Attended).
4. Fixed First Time? Or Quote
Fixed: straight to Works Done.
Needs more: price it (Quote), send it, client approves (Approved), book the works in, attend.
Scaffold / other trades and On Hold are flags on the job, not extra stages.
5. Works Done
Completion photos and signature. The PM checks it: it's ready to invoice.
6. Invoiced → Paid (Finance)
Raise from the job (or Invoice Done Jobs for the month): the price and details fill in. The job ticks Invoiced, then Paid when the money's in.
All The Way Through
Costs booked to the contract, then the job (like plots). Notes the client can see. Everything shows on the progress bar.
