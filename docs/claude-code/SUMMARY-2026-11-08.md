# Summary for Andy: 8 November 2026 (evening)

Everything below is in the new app and committed. **Press Push origin in GitHub Desktop** to send it to GitHub.

## Repair jobs

**The Works Orders boxes** (Projects and Finance, phone and laptop)
- The boxes are all the same size.
- Tapping a box opens its jobs **on a page of their own**, with a drop-down at the top (e.g. "Quotation Required (1) ▾") to jump to another box. Back takes you to the boxes.
- The jobs are in a **table**, as other lists are: Order No, Address, Booked on a phone; more columns on the laptop (Stage, Operative, Due).
- The path at the top shows where you are, e.g. Live Jobs › Quotation Required › W00619846. Each step is tappable.
- **The footer bar** is one line: the number of jobs and Export Excel. Schedule Of Rates and Costs By Job are now **tabs** in Finance: Live Jobs | Costs By Job | Schedule Of Rates.

**A job's page**
- **Client and Works Order side by side.** The works order shows one heading, the works order number and "View ›".
- **Booking Date** opens a pop-up: date, time slot, operative and notes for the booking. The calendar shows the slot, and the operative sees the slot and notes.
- **Attended** opens **Update Status**: date, who attended, fault found, repair needed and the photos. Attended By fills itself with the operative who uploaded the visit photos, and stays empty if a PM uploaded them. PMs get **Delete Visit**.
- The Repair tab is now **Work Summary**. It has **‹ Job Page** beside Update ▾ to go back.
- Reports and photos show on the Job tab once they're in.
- Tenant is now **Resident** everywhere. Attendance Report is now **Booking Visit Report**.

**Money (Finance), per works order**
- Three tiles: **Quoted**, **Costs** and **Profit** (with the margin %). Each opens a pop-up.
  - Quoted shows net, VAT, total, when it was sent and the client's answer, with Open The Quote.
  - Costs / Profit shows materials, labour, plant, subcontract and other costs, anything ordered but not invoiced, the profit and the margin.
- From that pop-up you can:
  - **See Every Cost**;
  - **+ Add A Cost**, which is booked to the job;
  - **Book A Cost To This Job**, which picks from the contract's costs not yet booked to a job.
- **Costs By Job** (a tab) shows every works order's costs, value and margin. Tap one to see its costs, book a cost to it, or add one.
- **Plots / multi-site jobs** get the same, as **Costs By Plot** under the Bill of Quantities tabs.

**The quote**
- One row: **📄 View Quote** on the left, then **Save** and **Send To Client** (which saves first).
- Both put the job in **Awaiting Client Approval**, and a line above the buttons says so. While Finance is in trial for RTB, Send To Client saves but holds the email back.
- **Quote Update ▾** on the Job and Work Summary tabs:
  - **Quote Approved**;
  - **Quote Rejected**: back to Quotation Required to price it again;
  - **Rejected And Close Job**: closes it for good, after a Yes/No.

**New Job**
- One button: **Upload Works Order/Orders**. One PDF fills in the form; several make a job each. Bulk Add is gone.

## OneDrive
- **One OneDrive page**, under Admin in Projects (Admin › OneDrive) and in Finance (Settings › OneDrive). It has:
  - the connection;
  - **New Jobs**: a folder for every new job? Yes/No, plus where they go and the template, chosen by browsing OneDrive;
  - **Where Things Save**, with Projects / Finance / Company tabs. Tap any folder to change it, use Yes/No to switch auto-save on or off, and **+ Add Something To Save** (e.g. Applications For Payment, Sales Invoices);
  - Quick Links, and each job's folders.
- **Add a Project** has one new tick box: "Create OneDrive folder".
- **Finance › Directory › a client:** "Create OneDrive Folders For This Client's Jobs? Yes/No".
- **Super admin:** each company on Platform Admin has a "Setting Up The Company" checklist.
- **The sign-in fix is live:** connecting OneDrive now brings you back to OpHUB, never to that page of code.
